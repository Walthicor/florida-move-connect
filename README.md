# Florida Movers

Create a modern, minimalist lead-generation website for Florida Movers Direct, a platform connecting Florida residents with licensed moving companies (up to 4 free quotes, always free for the user). This is a rebuild of an existing high-SEO site, so the URL structure I give you is sacred.
DESIGN SYSTEM: Colors: primary deep blue #0B5394, accent orange #F97316 for ALL call-to-action buttons, dark text #1F2937, alternating light section background #F4F7FB, footer navy #0B2540. Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans bold for headings, Inter for body. Generous white space, 8px radius on buttons/cards, subtle shadows, smooth scrolling, mobile-first (90% of traffic is mobile). Style: premium, clean, trustworthy — modern fintech feel applied to moving services.
GLOBAL HEADER (sticky): FMD logo left · nav: Services (dropdown to all 8 service pages), Locations (dropdown to all 7 city pages), Blog, Contact · EN/ES language toggle · orange button: Get Free Quotes.
GLOBAL FOOTER (navy #0B2540, white text, 4 columns): 1) Logo + line: Compare free quotes from licensed Florida movers. FDACS and FMCSA verified. 2) Services links. 3) Cities links. 4) Contact + links to Privacy Policy and Terms.
Every page ends with a full-width orange CTA band: headline Ready to Move? Compare Free Quotes in 2 Minutes + button.
CREATE THESE EXACT ROUTES (trailing slashes matter, each its own page, placeholder content for now): / , /local-movers-in-florida/ , /long-distance-movers/ , /storage-solutions/ , /commercial-movers/ , /packing-services/ , /piano-movers/ , /apartment-movers/ , /movers-in-miami/ , /movers-in-tampa/ , /movers-in-orlando/ , /movers-in-ocala/ , /movers-in-jacksonville/ , /movers-in-naples/ , /movers-in-boca-raton/ , /piano-movers-tampa/ , /contact/ , /blog/
Also create redirect routes: /miami-movers/ redirects to /movers-in-miami/ ; /local-movers/ redirects to /local-movers-in-florida/ ; /moving-storage/ redirects to /storage-solutions/ . Build design system, header, footer and routing now — content comes next.
